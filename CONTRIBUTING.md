# Contributing

Corrections to the map data are welcome. The quickest route is the map itself: go to the year and place that looks
wrong and press the flag button, **Report an error**, or write to hello@thewheeleratlas.com. A source helps a great deal:
a historical atlas, a scholarly book or article, or a primary document. Accepted corrections are listed, with their
source, on the public corrections log at https://thewheeleratlas.com/corrections.

Pull requests are also welcome. Please say what you changed and give the source for it.
