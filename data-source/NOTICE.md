## What was changed (GPL-3.0 §5a)

The era and overlay files in `../site/` were produced from the upstream GeoJSON by the scripts in `scripts/`, with these changes:
- The polygons were rasterized onto a Miller-projection grid (4096 × 2111) and snapped to Natural Earth coastlines. Small coastal gaps were filled.
- `scripts/era_edits.py` applies hand corrections for each year. Realms are split, merged, renamed, re-dated, added, removed and re-coloured, and borders are corrected with boxes and polygons.
- Later corrections (2026), including new 1210, 1450 and 1750 maps and fixes to 1200, 1453, 1900, 1914 and 1945, were made as data records. Their GPL records are in `hb-gpl/`, and their results are in `../site/eras/`.
- Unclaimed land is filled by carrying peoples forward or backward from neighbouring years.
- A 2026 snapshot was added from Natural Earth admin-0 boundaries (public domain).
- The religion, culture and development layers in `../site/rel/`, `../site/cul/` and `../site/dev/` are painted onto this border data by the rules and scripts in `scripts/overlays/`.

- October 2026: name-only corrections (for example Kamakura shogunate, Qing Empire, Afsharid Iran, colonial names in 1945) applied by `scripts/fixes-2026/fix_batch3.py`, `fix_batch4.py` and `fix_batch5.py` (22 October: misspelt, garbled and out-of-date realm names). Border shapes were not changed by these. `scripts/fixes-2026/fix_1530_ottoman.py` (22 October) moved the 1530 map's leftover Mamluk territory (Barqa) and an Aq Qoyunlu sliver into the Ottoman Empire; their shapes are unchanged. `scripts/derive-2026/make_117.py` (23 October) derives a new 117 CE map from the 100 CE map: Dacia, Arabia Petraea and Armenia join the Roman Empire and Mesopotamia is carved from Parthia. `scripts/fixes-2026/fix_1530_iraq.py` (23 October) returns Iraq, Azerbaijan and western Iran to the Safavid Empire on the 1530 map; borders are re-traced with the same code. `scripts/fixes-2026/fix_1530_hasa.py` (24 October) gives al-Hasa, Ottoman only from 1550, to the Jabrid Sultanate on the 1530 map. `scripts/fixes-2026/fix_slivers.py` (25 October) gives tiny stray pieces of realms (up to 3 map pixels) to the territory around them on every map, removes leftover Kalmar Union and Denmark-Norway fragments (1492, 1500, 1530) and the not-yet-founded crusader states (1066), corrects Hanover and Mecklenburg-Strelitz (1783, 1800), and splits Holstein and the Mecklenburg duchies out of a territory labelled Lübeck (1715, 1750). `scripts/fixes-2026/fix_hre_split.py` (25 October) gives Bavaria, Saxony, Württemberg, the Electoral Palatinate and the other larger German states their own territories inside the Holy Roman Empire on the 1650–1750 maps, using outlines from the 1715, 1783, 1800 and 1815 maps, and corrects Silesia and Bavaria on the 1783 map and the realms of Hanover and Mecklenburg-Strelitz on the 1800 map. `scripts/fixes-2026/fix_hre_1600.py` (25 October) does the same for the 1600 map, with the Habsburg lands, Brandenburg, Pomerania and the Upper Palatinate cut to their 1600 extent. `scripts/derive-2026/make_bc550.py` (23 October) derives a new 550 BCE map from the 500 BCE map: the Achaemenid lands become Media, Lydia, the Neo-Babylonian Empire, Saite Egypt and their neighbours. `scripts/derive-2026/make_bc525.py` (23 October) derives a 525 BCE map from it: those lands become the Achaemenid Empire of Cyrus and Cambyses. `scripts/derive-2026/make_bc250.py` (24 October) derives a 250 BCE map from the 300 BCE map: Rome takes Italy south of the Arno and most of Sicily (with Syracuse as its ally), the Diadochi kingdoms become Antigonid Macedon, the Seleucid and Ptolemaic kingdoms, Pergamon, Bithynia and Pontus, the Mauryan Empire reaches the Tamil kingdoms, and Qin takes Shangdang, Luoyang and the Han valley. On 26 October `scripts/fixes-2026/fix_overlay_overseas.py` corrects the culture and religion layers where they had carried a modern country's people and faith onto its overseas parts or onto islands before anyone lived there: French Guiana, Guadeloupe, Martinique, Réunion, Mayotte and Bonaire before European settlement; the Azores, Madeira, Galápagos, Svalbard, Canaries, Easter Island, Falklands and Bermuda, now n/a until settled; the Andaman religion; Caribbean small-island religion after colonisation; the Karelian Isthmus 1300–1938; and a coastal hunter-gatherer and animism rim on small island states from 1700. It also recomputes the area totals in `../site/data/cultures.json` and `religions.json` and the affected main values in `../site/data/ovmain.json`. The new n/a areas are listed in `NA-REGISTER.csv`. `scripts/fixes-2026/fix_overlay_judgement.py` (26 October) makes judgement calls on the culture layer: Early Slavs instead of Bulgarians, Serbs, Croats and Slovenes on the 600 map (and in North Macedonia on the 700 and 800 maps); Mande, then Fula, for the kingdom of Kaabu in eastern Guinea-Bissau; Chibchan peoples on Panama's Caribbean coast from 500 BCE and a Spanish-colonial isthmus from 1530; Coahuiltecan, Apache, Comanche and then American peoples in south-central Texas instead of the Caddo; German in Danzig, Elbing and the Vistula delta from 1400 to 1938; and "Serbian" renamed "Serbian and Montenegrin". It recomputes the areas in `../site/data/cultures.json` and the affected main values in `../site/data/ovmain.json`. `scripts/fixes-2026/fix_realm_qc.py` (26 October) corrects realms shown outside their time: the Guanche Canary Islands are Castilian, then Spanish, from 1500; Hong Kong joins the Qing Empire on the 1650-1800 maps; names that did not fit their date are renamed (European hunter-gatherers and Neolithic farmers before the Bronze Age, the Northern European Iron Age from 550 BCE, the Susiana and the Halaf, Ubaid and Gawra cultures instead of Elam and the Hurrian kingdoms in 5000-4000 BCE, Saint-Domingue, the Malay sultanates and the Somali sultanates); and Brittany, Burgundy, Rajasthan and The Gambia are spelled correctly. Judgement calls in the same script name the Wolof and Fula kingdoms instead of "Senegal" (1530-1815) and the Bay of Honduras settlements instead of "Belize" (1650-1750), leave the Turks and Caicos Islands unclaimed on the 1530-1650 maps, and name the Nordic Bronze Age on the 1500 BCE map. `scripts/fixes-2026/fix_realm_qc2.py` (26 October) shows Korea as Goryeo (1279, 1300) and Joseon (1400) instead of part of the Yuan, Java and Bali as Majapahit and the Sunda Kingdom instead of the Malay sultanates (1450-1530), names the Danish realm of 1000 Denmark (with Norway), and removes a one-pixel leftover of Alexander's empire on the 250 BCE map. `scripts/fixes-2026/fix_overlay_qc2.py` (26 October) names early medieval Europe for its time on the culture layer (Gallo-Romans, Germanic tribes and Latin Italy instead of French, German, Dutch and Italian on the 500-800 maps) and makes uninhabited Arctic islands n/a (Franz Josef Land, Severnaya Zemlya and the New Siberian Islands on every map, Novaya Zemlya before 1871, Wrangel Island before 1926). The new n/a areas are listed in `NA-REGISTER.csv`.

These modifications were made by Gregory D Wheeler in 2026 and last revised in October 2026. The 331 pre-launch corrections are part of this GPL work; they are the `notes` arrays in `../site/eras/*.json`. The public corresponding source is https://github.com/thewheeleratlas/atlas-data.

## Other inputs (public domain, not included)

Natural Earth 1:10m layers (land, lakes, glaciers, rivers, admin-0, admin-1). Fetch them with `scripts/fetch_natural_earth.py`.
