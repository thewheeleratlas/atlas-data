## What was changed (GPL-3.0 §5a)

The era and overlay files in `../site/` were produced from the upstream GeoJSON by the scripts in `scripts/`, with these changes:
- The polygons were rasterized onto a Miller-projection grid (4096 × 2111) and snapped to Natural Earth coastlines. Small coastal gaps were filled.
- `scripts/era_edits.py` applies hand corrections for each year. Realms are split, merged, renamed, re-dated, added, removed and re-coloured, and borders are corrected with boxes and polygons.
- Later corrections (2026), including new 1210, 1450 and 1750 maps and fixes to 1200, 1453, 1900, 1914 and 1945, were made as data records. Their GPL records are in `hb-gpl/`, and their results are in `../site/eras/`.
- Unclaimed land is filled by carrying peoples forward or backward from neighbouring years.
- A 2026 snapshot was added from Natural Earth admin-0 boundaries (public domain).
- The religion, culture and development layers in `../site/rel/`, `../site/cul/` and `../site/dev/` are painted onto this border data by the rules and scripts in `scripts/overlays/`.

- October 2026: name-only corrections (for example Kamakura shogunate, Qing Empire, Afsharid Iran, colonial names in 1945) applied by `scripts/fixes-2026/fix_batch3.py`, `fix_batch4.py` and `fix_batch5.py` (22 October: misspelt, garbled and out-of-date realm names). Border shapes were not changed by these. `scripts/fixes-2026/fix_1530_ottoman.py` (22 October) moved the 1530 map's leftover Mamluk territory (Barqa) and an Aq Qoyunlu sliver into the Ottoman Empire; their shapes are unchanged. `scripts/derive-2026/make_117.py` (23 October) derives a new 117 CE map from the 100 CE map: Dacia, Arabia Petraea and Armenia join the Roman Empire and Mesopotamia is carved from Parthia. `scripts/fixes-2026/fix_1530_iraq.py` (23 October) returns Iraq, Azerbaijan and western Iran to the Safavid Empire on the 1530 map; borders are re-traced with the same code. `scripts/fixes-2026/fix_1530_hasa.py` (24 October) gives al-Hasa, Ottoman only from 1550, to the Jabrid Sultanate on the 1530 map. `scripts/fixes-2026/fix_slivers.py` (25 October) gives tiny stray pieces of realms (up to 3 map pixels) to the territory around them on every map, removes leftover Kalmar Union and Denmark-Norway fragments (1492, 1500, 1530) and the not-yet-founded crusader states (1066), corrects Hanover and Mecklenburg-Strelitz (1783, 1800), and splits Holstein and the Mecklenburg duchies out of a territory labelled Lübeck (1715, 1750). `scripts/fixes-2026/fix_hre_split.py` (25 October) gives Bavaria, Saxony, Württemberg, the Electoral Palatinate and the other larger German states their own territories inside the Holy Roman Empire on the 1650–1750 maps, using outlines from the 1715, 1783, 1800 and 1815 maps, and corrects Silesia and Bavaria on the 1783 map and the realms of Hanover and Mecklenburg-Strelitz on the 1800 map. `scripts/fixes-2026/fix_hre_1600.py` (25 October) does the same for the 1600 map, with the Habsburg lands, Brandenburg, Pomerania and the Upper Palatinate cut to their 1600 extent. `scripts/derive-2026/make_bc550.py` (23 October) derives a new 550 BCE map from the 500 BCE map: the Achaemenid lands become Media, Lydia, the Neo-Babylonian Empire, Saite Egypt and their neighbours. `scripts/derive-2026/make_bc525.py` (23 October) derives a 525 BCE map from it: those lands become the Achaemenid Empire of Cyrus and Cambyses. `scripts/derive-2026/make_bc250.py` (24 October) derives a 250 BCE map from the 300 BCE map: Rome takes Italy south of the Arno and most of Sicily (with Syracuse as its ally), the Diadochi kingdoms become Antigonid Macedon, the Seleucid and Ptolemaic kingdoms, Pergamon, Bithynia and Pontus, the Mauryan Empire reaches the Tamil kingdoms, and Qin takes Shangdang, Luoyang and the Han valley. `scripts/derive-2026/make_1989.py` (27 October) derives a new 1989 map from the 1994 map: the USSR, Yugoslavia and Czechoslovakia are whole again, Germany and Yemen are divided along the 1960 map's lines, Eritrea is part of Ethiopia, Namibia is South West Africa under South Africa, the West Bank and Gaza are occupied territories of Israel's realm, small states the source map leaves unclaimed (Singapore, Bahrain, the Maldives, Mauritius, the Seychelles, the Comoros, Cape Verde, São Tomé and Príncipe, the Solomon Islands, Vanuatu, Kiribati) and Socotra are added with the 2026 map's outlines, and dependencies shown as states join their governing countries. `scripts/derive-2026/make_1975.py` (27 October) derives a 1975 map from it: North and South Vietnam (the 1960 line), Rhodesia, Spanish Sahara, the Sinai under Israeli occupation, East Timor, and the British and French colonies and protectorates of the time. `scripts/derive-2026/cold_war_layers.py` paints their religion, culture and development layers with the rules in `scripts/overlays/`, carrying over the 2026 hand corrections of the 1960 and 1994 layers.

These modifications were made by Gregory D Wheeler in 2026 and last revised in October 2026. The 331 pre-launch corrections are part of this GPL work; they are the `notes` arrays in `../site/eras/*.json`. The public corresponding source is https://github.com/thewheeleratlas/atlas-data.

## Other inputs (public domain, not included)

Natural Earth 1:10m layers (land, lakes, glaciers, rivers, admin-0, admin-1, populated places). Fetch them with `scripts/fetch_natural_earth.py`.
